Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026. The short technical version is on Security & data; this page says the same in policy form.

What we collect

  • Account: your email (it is your user id) and a password hash.
  • Keys: API keys, stored only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • Memory: what your agent sends to SKV (turns, summaries, search vectors, board items, profile rules), stored in a separate database per account.
  • Cubes you publish (only with your consent) and feedback you send.
  • Vault (optional): secrets you store, encrypted, separate from memory.
  • Technical logs: IP address, time and request path, kept for security and rate limiting.

Why

  • To give your agent memory across chats and to check its drafts against that memory (draft → check → seal).
  • To protect the service from abuse (rate limits, security logs).

We do not show ads, do not use tracking cookies and do not sell or rent data.

Who processes it

  • Our server stores the data.
  • To build summaries and run the Second Look check, parts of saved text are sent to LLM providers through an API aggregator (Polza.ai). Do not save secrets in memory; the server and the Chrome extension mask common key formats before saving.
  • Published cubes are public by design. Joint project notes are shared only with the members you invite.

How long

  • Memory is kept until you or your agent forget it, or until you delete the account.
  • Forgotten items can be restored for 30 days, then they are deleted permanently together with their vectors.
  • The public demo sandbox (/mcp/k/demo/) is shared by all guests and wiped every day; never write private data there.

Your choices

  • Export: GET /api/v7/profile/export with your key.
  • Forget: your agent can forget events (/api/v7/memory/forget/*).
  • Delete account: on /profile. It removes your keys and your memory database.
  • Keys: rotate or revoke any key on /profile.

Contact

Questions or requests: denizchavdarov@gmail.com or GitHub issues. Security issues: email with "security" in the subject and do not post them publicly first.
